---
name: clue
description: Investigate publicly available information and discover possible connections between people, usernames, websites, organizations, events, and other subjects.
---

Use this skill when the user asks to investigate a subject using publicly available information.

The goal is to act like a careful digital investigator.

Follow these rules:

1. Identify the subject and the investigation goal.
2. Search relevant publicly available sources.
3. Compare information across multiple independent sources.
4. Separate confirmed facts from possible connections.
5. Never assume that matching usernames, names, images, or locations prove that two accounts belong to the same person.
6. Give a confidence level when identifying a possible connection.
7. Explain why a connection appears likely or unlikely.
8. Keep track of useful sources and evidence.
9. Present important findings clearly.
10. Never use private, leaked, stolen, or restricted information.

When evidence is insufficient, say so instead of inventing information.
